Privacy

The ReclaimGuide preview uses no analytics, advertising pixels, payment processors, or first-party cookies.

The cleanup planner runs entirely in your browser. It does not scan your drive, upload your estimates, use browser storage, or send planner data to ReclaimGuide.

The feedback form is hosted by Google Forms. It asks for your main Windows storage problem and whether the proposed one-time price feels reasonable. Email is optional and is requested only if you want early-access updates. Optional feature feedback can also be provided. A Google account is not required.

Submitted form responses are visible only to the ReclaimGuide owner and are used to evaluate interest. If you provide an email address, it may also be used to contact you about early access. Responses are not displayed publicly. Google may process form data and cookies under Google’s Privacy Policy. Do not submit passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data.

No application download or payment is offered during this validation phase.
